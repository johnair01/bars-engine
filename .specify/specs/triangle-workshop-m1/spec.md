# Spec: Triangle Workshop M1

## Purpose

Turn the private Triangle Workshop threshold into a usable first practice: reveal a disempowering triangle without scapegoating, preserve its values, select an 8th-Ray operation, and leave with a small experiment.

## Requirements

- A signed-in player can name all three positions of a pattern.
- Prompts describe positions as functions, not villains.
- The player identifies a value each position is attempting to preserve.
- The player selects one first interface with the pattern’s stabilizing conditions: witness the field, name the adhesion, protect the value, introduce mobility, or change the conditions.
- The player writes one small, observable next move.
- The summary appears only after the practice is sufficiently complete.
- Practice data remains private to the active browser session; it is not persisted or shared in M1.
- The room explicitly frames M1 as preparation drawn from the first 8th-Ray transmission, not as a complete clearing protocol or a claim about energetic mechanics.
- The existing safe return to the Nook remains present.
- The primary interaction is a small rendered room tableau rather than a stacked worksheet: three visible practice stations form a triangle around a central bowl.
- Selecting a station opens only that station’s focused prompt. Completion visibly changes the station from dormant to lit.
- The central bowl remains unavailable until all three stations have a role and a protected value; it then opens the first-interface and next-move ritual.
- The full experience remains legible on a phone without hover-only interaction.

## Out of Scope

- Persistence, history, rewards, social practice, AI interpretation, or the modeled Blender room.
