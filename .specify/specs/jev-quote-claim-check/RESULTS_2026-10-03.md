## T1: what TypeSafe's docs say (read today)

- **Noul** (`docs.typesafe.ai/primitives/noul.md`): returns one probability from 0 to 1, with no separate confidence. It says to ask one question per Noul, to threshold the value in code, and to route values between roughly 0.3 and 0.7 to a human. It says that if the question is really about degree, the value does not measure the degree.
- **Citation check cookbook**: one Choice with three options (supports, contradicts, says nothing), after an exact string match on the quote. Its own test used eight citations, four accurate and four planted failures, and caught all four, with the planted ones at 0.27 to 0.99. Eight is too few to tell us anything about our material. The cookbook says a truncated or lightly reworded quote comes back as fabricated, and sets its auto-accept threshold at 0.8.
- **Not stated anywhere I opened:** pricing, rate limits, input size limits and accuracy on text like ours. The legal page's index says the Privacy Policy commits not to train models on user data and that enterprise customers can have zero data retention. The retention terms are in documents I did not open (task T6a).
