doorway opens, and all three lands of the episode walk: the clearing's broken bar is the tune of the work they named,
the Scale-House's balance weighs what they hoard, and the friend they named waits at the fire. Today only the sample
player's charge does this; a charge the player writes gives lands with nothing in them, because its free text was
never drafted.
