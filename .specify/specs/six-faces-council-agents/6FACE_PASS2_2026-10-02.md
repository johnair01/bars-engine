# Six faces, pass two — *questions are not the product*

**Convened 2026-10-02, after pass one.** Called by Wendell on pass one's own ending, which handed him six questions. His
brief, in his words:

> *"The idea is that we can recursively call the game masters to solve for exactly these problems of
> constant reaching for me. We can do ontological Socratic intakes so I can teach the agents to be
> smart by interfacing with their output but most of the time their output is more questions.*
>
> *Questions and interfaceable product are two separate things and I want to be working in interfaces
> more than I want in chat. Chat should be for providing extra context for the agents and steering
> them in the right direction."*

**Pass one is the specimen.** It resolved six decisions in its own body and then asked Wendell to
ratify every one. This pass has to do better than describe the failure. It has to land in an
interface, or it has failed the same way twice.

## Anchor

The council exists to make Wendell's decisions better at the place he makes them. He has now said
where that place is: an interface, with chat for context and steering. A question is not a product.

---

## 🧠 Architect — structure

**The council has two outputs and the spec typed them as one.** A pass produces *product*: a verdict,
a synthesis, a spec delta, a page. It also produces *questions*. Pass one mixed them in one landing,
so the questions arrived as prose and the product arrived as something for Wendell to approve.
**`Consult` gets a `questions` field, separate from `landing`, and a question is a typed object**: the
face that owns it, the options, the consequence of each option, and one line saying why only Wendell
can answer it.

**The recursion is a loop with a stopping rule.** Before a question leaves the council, the Sage sends
it back to the face that owns it with the record: the decision log, the working rules, prior passes.
The face answers with a citation, or it says what is missing. **A question reaches Wendell only when
what is missing is his preference, or a fact only he holds.** Everything else the council answers and
cites. The depth is bounded, and the bound is the Sage's to set and cheap to change.

**The interface in the Claude flow exists today.** An artifact page with a store: the council writes
its positions, Wendell flips any he disagrees with and types a steer, and the next session reads the
store back as data. In bars-engine the native form is a CYOA quest, the shape the certification
quests already use, and that comes later. Same data, two surfaces. The third position from pass one
holds: the council is a prompt pack and a record schema, and the interface renders the record.

## 🏛 Regent — what to preserve

**The ruling is still his.** Recursion reduces what reaches him. It never answers a reserved question
on his behalf. The reserved list from pass one stands, with the Regent's addition.

**A click on a page is a conscious approval when the page says what the click does.** The working
rule in friendcraft is that no canonical write happens without Wendell's conscious approval. A board
row that reads *stands* beside a position he can flip is that approval, in a form he can act on
without a follow-up question. A silent default with no page behind it is not.

**An auto-answer with no citation is a reach wearing an answer's clothes.** This is the Muhani tell
from the working rules, applied to the loop: a face that answers a question "from the record" has
to name the entry. No citation, no answer, and the question goes to the board.

**Every board answer lands in the decision log.** The board is where he answers. The log is where the
answer is kept. The skill writes the log entry when it reads the board.

## ⚔️ Challenger — what gets wrong

**Run the reach test on pass one's six questions and name the score.** Which six is canonical: every
Wendell-convened pass used the altitude six, so the record answered it. The model: the anchor
answered it. The build order: the pass itself resolved it and then asked him to agree. The ledger
location: the Architect answered it with a fact about repos. The reserved list: a rule exists and
adding to it is the Sage's job. The run length: his by the Numbers rule, and the Challenger had
already said not to set it until ten rulings exist, so it was not due. **Zero of six needed him.** The
pass manufactured six asks out of deference, and deference is the reach.

**A Socratic intake can recurse forever.** Ouroboros stops at an ambiguity score, and that score is a
number somebody picked. The honest stop is plainer: **a question reaches Wendell only if his answer
changes what gets built.** If every option leads to the same next step, the council picks one, cites
why, and moves.

**An interface is not better than chat by being an interface.** A board with forty toggles is chat with
worse affordances. Each row carries the position, the citation, two states and a steer box, and a
row that cannot be written that tightly goes back to its face.

**The intakes are his own method and the faces did not invent them.** The six unpacking questions,
the 3-2-1, the flirtcraft intake. The council borrows the shape. It should say so and not dress it as
new.

## 🎭 Diplomat — what bridges

**Chat and interface now have different jobs, and the copy follows.** Chat carries context and
steering, in his words. The interface carries answers. So the chat half of a pass shrinks to what
changed and where to act, and the pass itself lives in the file and on the board.

**Each board row says why it is being asked, and why it was not asked before.** That is the flirtcraft
intake rule, from his own brief on 2026-09-09, and it applies to him as much as to a stranger at the
paywall. A row without that line is a form field.

**The steer box is how he teaches the agents.** His sentence beside a position becomes a lesson in
the definition file for the face that owned it. That is the loop closing in the direction he asked
for: his words into the faces, not his time into their questions.

**The board is also the door for a second person.** A collaborator who cannot open his Claude session
can open a page. The MCP door from pass one stays, and the board arrives first.

## 🌊 Shaman — felt sense

**What is moving underneath is the same fatigue as pass one, named more exactly.** Being asked to be
the oracle for what the record already holds. An open question is a demand on his attention. A row
with options and a default is an offer he can decline.

**The intake is receiving, not grading.** The board asks what he prefers. It never scores him, and it
never scores a face in a way that reads as a verdict on a person. The §2c test holds here because
the only number on the board is a count of rows.

**The felt risk in recursion is smoothness.** A loop that answers its own questions will produce
answers that read settled. The citation rule is the guard, and the Shaman adds one more: **a row the
council answered for itself is shown on the board, marked as answered, so he can see what was
decided without him and flip it.** Hidden auto-answers are the silent decision pass one warned about,
wearing a different coat.

## 📖 Sage — panoramic

**The whole is a loop with two typed outputs and one stopping rule.** Product and questions, typed
apart. A question goes back to its face with the record before it goes anywhere else. It reaches
Wendell only with options, consequences, a citation for why it is his, and a line saying why it was
not asked before. It reaches him on a board, with a default he can flip and a steer box whose text
becomes a lesson.

**Pass one's six, re-run through the loop, produce zero questions.** They produce ten positions,
each with its citation, and those are what the board shows. The board also shows the positions this
pass resolved, marked as answered without him.

**The spec changes in three places.** `Consult` gains `questions` and `positions`. The build order
from pass one gains the board as step two, beside the skill. The chat reply of a pass becomes what
changed and a link.

## Where the pass lands

**It lands on a board rather than in a question.** The council's positions from both passes are on a page Wendell can
open. Each row shows the position, the citation for why it did not need him, a state he can flip
from *stands* to *overrule*, and a steer box. A context box at the top takes anything he wants the
agents to carry. The next session reads the store as data and writes what he changed into the
decision log and into the faces' lessons.

**The chat half of this pass is the link and one line of what changed.** The rest is in this file.
