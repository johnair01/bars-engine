3 Face it third person · they

Describe the part as “they.” What are they trying to do? What do they keep watching for? How long have they been doing this job?

2 Talk to it second person · you · a thread

Before you speak to them, what do you call them? Not a job title — a name that fits how they work.

Now go back and forth. Ask what they are protecting, switch voices, and let them answer in their own words. Keep going until they say something you did not already know.
