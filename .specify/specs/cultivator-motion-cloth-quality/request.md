# Request: cultivator motion and cloth quality

On 2026-11-12 in chat, Wendell asked: “Let's create a spec kit for how best with our current resources and capacity improve the quality of these models. and then we'll work on getting the cloth right. I also need to see how the different body sized we accepted in ‘your looks’ are applied to these runs.”

The immediate user is Wendell reviewing the cultivator in motion. The player is the eventual wearer of the chosen look. The current run and jump can be inspected, but a passing capture has repeatedly failed to predict whether weight, contact, and cloth read convincingly. The review viewer also exposes only average builds, even though the character recipe accepts more.

This request extends the existing B2 cultivator and motion lab. It creates a repeatable quality loop and body-size review before revising the robe. It does not authorize a new avatar system or a general physics engine.

Record used: chat notes saved in `public/between/cult/review-notes.jsonl`; the council positions `cr-first-menu` and `cult-parts-height` in the council's decided board record; the current character recipe in `public/between/cult/make/parts.json`. The board page inspected on 2026-11-12 reported its pushed content as about three hours old, so newer board material must be reconciled before claiming full council agreement.
