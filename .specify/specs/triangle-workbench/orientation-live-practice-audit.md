## What is directly useful for product design

1. **Familiarity precedes fluent use.** Session 17 describes mechanics as something already built, after which one can simply use them. Our product translation: explain and rehearse Thought String grammar before asking a player to use it on their BAR.
2. **Practice is exploratory, not examinable.** All four playbooks emphasize play, curiosity, no hurry, and no wrong result. Orientation should have small reversible experiments and no pass/fail state.
3. **Creation has an arrival.** Sessions 16–18 repeatedly use present-time orientation, intention, a word platform, and deliberate attention before the creative work. Our translation: a compact live-practice arrival moment before a consequential operation.
4. **Clear and create are separate moves.** Session 18 explicitly pairs work on an unsupportive pattern with weaving/constructing a desired pattern. Our translation: never make “Apply 8th Ray” the entire loop; the player can then extend/build/bridge toward a positive continuation or settle without forcing a replacement.
5. **The environment is an interface.** Sessions 16–18 use the Cube as a creative environment/interface. Our translation: the dojo room is not a decorative dashboard; wall, stools, and bowl should each make a practice move legible.

## Product guardrails

- We do not represent the app as proving or causing metaphysical effects.
- We use source concepts as a contemplative-practice grammar: attention, intention, word construction, observation, deliberate change, and reflection.
- Tutorial material cannot generate embers, level spirits, classify daemons, update BARs, or create Between offers.
- Live practice is private by default and may be discarded or settled at every stage.
