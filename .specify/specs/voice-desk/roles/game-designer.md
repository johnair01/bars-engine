# Game designer: the Teaching Desk's loop and its numbers

Role: game-designer (ccgs-roles, council/toolbox.yaml), 2026-11-17, reading pass voice-forge-1 and the four mechanics.

**The loop's unit is the slip, and the session's unit is five.** A slip is one shipped line. Five slips fit the
Short Sitting promise of about five minutes: a minute a slip is one stop or two, three turns and a keep. Five is also
the handoff's number; the build reads it from one constant so a tuning-bench change can move it, but no agent moves it
without his word.

**Every slip has three clean exits.** Keep (one press), write his own (the blank fourth card), hatch (a sweep). None of
them is a fail. The hatch's ten seconds at the lip (compost-hatch forgiveness) is the undo; there is no other undo
button, so the desk never asks "are you sure".

**Waving on is the default.** The apprentice stops at each computed stop, but a tap anywhere that is not a held finger
moves it along. A sitting where he rings nothing and keeps the first redraft every time is a fast, valid sitting, and
it still teaches: five kept lines in the bank.

**What rewards him is the world, not a meter.** No counter, streak or score appears (FR-027). The feedback is the
apprentice's next reading (fewer stops on a screen whose words have stones), the sealed line rising to the top of the
book, and later the build passing on a screen that used to fail.

**The stone can wait.** A ringed word with no stone goes to the ink stone after the slip, never during it, so the
slip's rhythm stays intact. Leaving a stone for later is one tap and costs nothing.

**Tuning.** Fader defaults, the five-slip count, the 0.65 s hold and the 10 s lip are all read from data, so the
tuning-bench can change them without code. The council's notch stays on every scale.
