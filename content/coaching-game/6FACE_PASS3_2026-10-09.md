## Anchor

A visitor arrives with something. If they can name it, they say it in their own words and where in their life it
sits, see how Wendell works, and walk to the centre. If they can't, the forest helps them find four answers, or the
ontology game finds two of them and hands them back: the emotional block, the belief at play, the life domain, and the
level of help, as one of the six faces. The centre gives back what they found, offers a strategy, and has the call.

## Shaman (14, Noon)

**The cast says everything is visible.** At the centre the visitor sees what they found, labelled plainly: what they
want help with, where in their life, what is in the way, the belief at play, the help they need. Nothing is hidden
behind a reading they have to decode.

**The walk keeps its ways through.** Every new step has a pass: "I can't tell yet" on the belief, "Everywhere, or I
can't say" on the domain, "I'm not sure" on the face. This is the Shaman's lesson of 9 September ("if there IS a block
we should assume self-sabotage, with the given choice to skip"). The belief step asks for self-sabotage directly, as
"When you try to move on this, what does a part of you say?", and offers the skip.

## Architect (18, Stale Air)

**The cast says something went off from being left alone.** Two parts of the site sat apart: the ontology game and
the coaching page. The game already ends with the channel and the face the player worked (`selectedChannel`,
`selectedFace` on its last screen), and its colour faces are the council's faces by altitude (council/faces.yaml).
So the hand-off is one link: on the site build, the game's last screen offers "Take this to coaching", which opens
`/coaching#from-game?channel=…&face=…`. The page reads it, says what came across, and picks up the walk at the belief.

**What does not cross.** The game never stores belief text (oag-map-private, 24 September), and a link's address
stays in browser history. So the belief stays in the game, and the coaching page asks for it again in the visitor's
words. This stands as `cf-game-belief`; if Wendell wants the belief carried, it is a one-line change.

**Life domains.** The record already has a list: sprout's `ya-life-domains` (relationships, career, money,
creativity, health, home, community), from his answer on `ya-domains` (5 October: "really it's about life domains,
Relationships, Career, Money, Creativity etc."). The page uses it as is, and it changes when the Lenses domains are
formalized.
