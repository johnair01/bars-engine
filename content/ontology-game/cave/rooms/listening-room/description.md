# The Listening Room (sample description)

---

You push open a heavy wooden door, iron-banded and swollen with damp, and step down into the Listening Room. It is an
oval chamber, perhaps ten paces long and seven wide, with a low domed ceiling you could almost touch with an
outstretched hand. Two squat stone lanterns flank the doorway, their warm light pooling on the packed-earth floor.

At the centre of the room sits a low round table of dark wood, ringed by three faded red cushions, as though someone
left mid-conversation. Above it, a paper lantern hangs from a pale root that has pushed through the ceiling, casting a
soft amber glow. More roots thread down from the dome, thin and searching, some brushing the floor.

Along the left wall, a colony of small mushrooms glows a cool blue-green, the only light in that half of the room. To
the right, a moss-covered boulder rests beside a shallow pool, perfectly still, reflecting the lantern above. A fern
grows from a crack at the water's edge.

At the far end, a narrow opening in the rock leads deeper into the dark. You can smell damp earth, and somewhere
water drips at a slow, steady rhythm, like a heartbeat.
