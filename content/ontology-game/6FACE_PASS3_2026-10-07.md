## Anchor

A player who hits resistance on a WAVE step should be able to work it in the order Wendell drew, come back to the
exact step, and keep a record of what the charge was, what resolved it and what they came to believe, so the practice
no longer has to be held in their head.

## Shaman (41, Spending)

**The cast names the cost before the move.** Each step of the sketch spends something of the player: attention, a
breath, an admission. The Shaman reads "Do EA practice" as the element being breathed with while it does its job, which
is what the game's opening practices already are (W.A.V.E., Simple Breaths, Sedona). The practice therefore belongs at
step 2, where Wendell drew it, and the belief the gate gives belongs at step 4.

**The daemon step needs consent built in.** The daemon canon says to enlist and never to fight. The step therefore asks
whether the daemon will step aside and accepts "Not yet" as an answer, thanks it, and lets the player go to the gate
with it standing beside them.

## Architect (9, Restraint)

**One small rule held the whole old order: the face screen asked the channel's job.** Moving the job to step 2 frees
the face pick to be the fourth step without rewriting the engine. Every new screen runs only while a block is open
(`blockStack.length > 0`), so the main cycle, the demo and the coach code are untouched. The steps are named in
`BLOCK_STEPS` and each screen shows where the player is in them, inside the card, because the trail bar keeps its
two-line limit (oag-trailhead).

**The Release is a screen, and the return is the existing return stack.** Release breathes once and then calls the
same `returnFromBlock` the game already used, so the size check and the route map keep working.

## Challenger (62, Overstep)

**The cast says the build is doing slightly too much, and the Challenger names where.** Two practices in one block (an
EA practice at step 2 and the old practice picker after holding the belief) would double the work. The hold at the
gate therefore goes straight to "Did it shift?" inside block work.

**The Challenger dissents on step 2.** It would run no practice at step 2 and keep the one practice after the belief,
so a block takes one practice and fewer screens. It accepts the majority reading because the sketch draws "Do EA
practice" on the element line, and it puts the choice to Wendell as oag-ea-practice.

## Regent (35, Sunrise)

**The record makes private text visible to storage, faster than the 24 September boundary planned.** That boundary
keeps belief text out of storage, and pass 1 and pass 2 extended it to the block's words. Wendell's message now asks
for a program that keeps track of beliefs. The Regent holds both by making the record a switch that starts off, keeps
everything in the player's browser, never enters the coach code, and can be erased in one tap. Whether that is the
right line is his call (oag-record).

## Diplomat (13, Fellowship)

**A newcomer has never met the daemons.** Each card gives the daemon's job from the book's essence rule, the step opens
with one sentence on what a daemon is, and "I'm not sure" is a card of its own, so the player can answer the questions
and let the answers name the daemon. The six faces are called game masters only at the gate, where the sketch names
them; the cards keep their plain names and colour tags (oag-faces-primer).

## Sage (10, Footing)

The Shaman placed the practice and the consent; the Architect kept the engine and the return; the Challenger cut the
second practice and dissented on step 2; the Regent drew the storage line; the Diplomat made the daemons legible. The
pass was not unanimous. The Challenger dissents on where the EA practice sits, and the Regent holds the record behind a
switch until Wendell rules on it. The Sage does not rule.

## Positions (each stands unless Wendell flips it on the board)

1. **oag-sketch-order.** Block work runs the sketch's steps in his order: 1 the blocker's sensation (the body scan),
   2 its element, how the element is doing its job, and an EA practice, 3 the daemon in the way and its job, 4 the six
   game masters' gate (pick the face, hold the belief it gives, see if it shifts), then Release, then the return to the
   step. *Why not asked:* the sketch and his message give the order. It also carries out his board answer on
   oag-block-work, "Mix of a and c": the game's own cycle with the daemon met.
2. **oag-daemon-step.** Step 3 offers the seven daemons with the book's essence line for each and an "I'm not sure"
   card, then asks what its job is right now (required), who it works for (optional), and whether it will step aside
   ("Yes, for now" or "Not yet", and either goes on to the gate). *Why not asked:* the seven and their essences are the
   book's canon (MTGOA Daemons, Breakdown; position bm-daemon-brief); the three questions are adapted from the interview
   in the Daemons from Big Mind proposal, without the Big Mind name.
3. **oag-gate.** The six game master gate is the face pick, made after the daemon, with the belief, the hold and the
   shift check behind it. Inside block work the gate does not ask the element's job again and runs no second practice.
   *Why not asked:* his message names the gate as the fourth step, and the six faces are the six game masters.
4. **oag-release.** A shift at the gate goes to a Release screen with one breath and the belief shown, then "Return to"
   the step. A block that does not shift keeps "Go back anyway". *Why not asked:* the sketch draws Release and the return.
5. **oag-restart.** Every W.A.V.E. Exhale offers "Still blocked? Start the W.A.V.E. again", which goes back to Welcome on
   the same charge and is logged on the route. *Why not asked:* the sketch draws "Restart until unblocked".
6. **oag-record.** A "Your record" screen, linked from the start and from Cycle Complete, lists every block worked
   (step, words, sensation, element and its job, practice, daemon and its job, the gate's face, the belief, released or
   not, size before and after) and every completed cycle (the start words, and each channel's face and belief). It is
   kept in this browser only, behind a switch that starts off, and has an Erase button. *Why not asked:* his message
   asks for tracking of charges, resolutions and beliefs. The switch is the council's choice; question oag-record asks
   whether it is the right line.

## Addendum: Wendell's board answers, 7 October 2026 (20:13 to 20:17)

Recorded in six-faces-council `council/ledger/2026-10-07-pull-203908.json`. The faces applied them without a new
pass, because each one either answers its own question or says what to build.

- **oag-ea-practice (steer).** His words: "I don't think one should do a WAVE after saying how the element does it's
  job. The EA practice should be one connected to the element they choose. Maybe this connects with WAVE, but it
  should be tools specifically made for that move. It needs to be an open up move so they can feel it more, and should
  be able to have the players mark if they are experiencing blocks re: opening up to that emotion". Step 2 now goes
  from the job questions to an "Open up to the [emotion]" screen. It offers two tools made for that emotion, taken from
  `docs/EMOTIONAL_ALCHEMY_TOOL_TAXONOMY.md` where a tool is rated strong for Open Up and strong for the channel:
  Anger, Return to the Body and Find the Felt Thread; Sadness, Find the Felt Thread and Make It Real; Fear, Find the
  Felt Thread and Return to the Body; Joy, Happy Apples and Make It Real; Neutrality, Find the Felt Thread and Happy
  Apples. Each tool shows three steps written to turn toward the emotion, and one breath. The player then marks
  anything in the way of opening up (five marks), and a marked block can be worked as a block of its own, which
  releases back to the same screen. The W.A.V.E. and practice picker no longer run at step 2.
- **oag-main-order (steer).** His words: "The whole game is based on WAVE based on my feedback and the feedback of
  people who've played it. Everything is downstream from wave so if WAVE is blocked one can diagnoze by digging deeper
  until the block releases and they can go back to the work that brought them to the practice in the first place".
  Every cycle now begins with W.A.V.E. (one "Begin with W.A.V.E." button; the random opener remains only for the test
  override and the demo). Inside block work, a belief that does not shift at the gate offers "Dig deeper", which opens
  a block inside the block; its Release comes back to the gate. The way back to the work that brought the player was
  already in place: each block returns to its step, and Cycle Complete returns to the start (oag-trailhead).
- **oag-daemon-step (overruled).** His words: "We should be telling them what the daemon's job is. They don't provide
  that info. Who it works for is also something that's supplied to them". The two inputs are gone. Picking a daemon
  shows its job (the book's essence line) and who it works for, from his ruling of 2 October 2026: "They are designed
  to protect the player but also all work for the players enjoyment". The step still asks whether it will step aside.
- **oag-record (answered A).** A switch that starts off, as built.
