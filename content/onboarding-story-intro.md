# Onboarding Story Intro

Canonical story-world intro copy for chained initiation, read by a brand-new player before character creation. Setting: the Heaven sect in Calrunia (council/callsheets/calrunian-sect.md in the council repo). Rewritten 2026-11-17 from Wendell's note on board row cd-words-swap-look-1.

## Principle

A new player who finishes these four cards can answer three questions. What is the Heaven sect? Why am I a member of it? What do I get from it? Each card says one thing in plain sentences. Each sentence names what a thing is. Two checks read this copy: scripts/copy-scan.py with the no-ai-slop scan.

## Intro Beats (for packet compiler)

### Beat 1 — Orientation: what the sect is, why you are here
**Welcome to the Heaven sect.** The sect is a school of cultivation on a mountain above the clouds. It takes in people who have left the frozen settlements below. You left one too. That is why its gate opened for you.

### Beat 2 — Rising Engagement: why it matters
**Cultivation here means training your emotional energy.** Every feeling carries a charge. In Calrunia that charge takes shape as spirits. A trained student can turn a stuck feeling into a clear next step. The same skill works on the stuck moments of your own week.

### Beat 3 — Tension: who teaches
**Six Elders teach in the sect.** Each Elder teaches one face of the game master. The Shaman reads feeling. The Architect builds structure. The Challenger tests the edge. The Regent keeps the rules. The Diplomat brings people together. The Sage sees the whole pattern.

### Beat 4 — Integration: what you get
**You start as an Initiate, the first of six ranks.** First you shape your character. Then you learn the basic moves, each one a short practice you can use today. Last, you choose the Elder whose way fits you. You leave with a starter share of vibeulons, the sect's coin of emotional energy.
