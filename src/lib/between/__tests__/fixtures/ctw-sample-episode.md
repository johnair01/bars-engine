## 2. Strong start (Diplomat)
The player steps out of the homeroom into the forest under rain and a high tide; the Weigher makes tools and resources heavy.

Every branch the player brushes hangs lower, and the toolbag on their back drags at the shoulders. Rain ticks on the leaves. Ahead, through the trees, a hum of music starts, and stops mid-bar. *(drafted)*
