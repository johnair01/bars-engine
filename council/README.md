# Six-faces council in this repo

The council's home moved to https://github.com/wendell-britt/six-faces-council on 2026-10-02, on
Wendell's ruling on the council board. This repo is now one council repo among others.

- `faces.yaml`, the skill, `hooks/council-sync.sh` and `tools/voice_lint.py` are synced copies. The
  hook refreshes them from the home repo when a session opens here. Change them in the home repo.
- `terms.yaml` and `ledger/` belong to this repo and never sync.
- The install, stats, backfill, harvest, lessons and packaging scripts, the cause categories, the
  registry and the board source now live in the home repo.
- The council's design history, its first four passes and the research behind the faces stay here,
  in `.specify/specs/six-faces-council-agents/`.
